About Quark

A trading journal that tells you the truth.

Quark turns every trade you log into a clear picture of your edge: where you make money, where you leak it, and what to fix first. Built for discretionary traders in Indian and global markets.

Institutional Calendar

Every session colour-coded by P&L, with weekly totals and one-tap trade logging.

Instrument Tracking

See which instruments pay you and which ones leak, each with its own calendar.

✨

AI Edge Audit

Execution score, expectancy, drawdown and your costliest mistakes, with one clear fix to work on.

Market News

Headlines for the symbols you actually trade, with saved stories and read tracking.

🚀

My Platforms

Your broker, charting and research sites one tap away, saved to your account.

🔒

Private by design

Sign in with email or Google. Database rules let each account read only its own journal.

How it works

1

Log a trade

Tap any day, add symbol, P&L, setup, mistake tag and a chart screenshot.

2

Review your edge

The calendar and instrument views show patterns across days, weeks and symbols.

3

Fix one leak

The audit ranks what is costing you most and gives a rule to follow next session.

Quark is a journaling and analytics tool, not investment advice. Audit figures are calculated on your device.
