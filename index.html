Quick glossary

Stop-loss
A price where you exit to cap a loss.
Risk : reward
Potential gain divided by potential loss on a trade.
OI (open interest)
Number of open option or futures contracts not yet closed.
PCR
Put-call ratio. Total put open interest divided by total call open interest.
ATR
Average true range. A measure of how much a price typically moves.
F&O
Futures and options, derivatives traded on the exchange.
Lot size
Fixed number of units in one derivatives contract.
STT
Securities Transaction Tax, charged on trades in India.

1. Read a candle

Each candle shows the open, high, low and close for one period. Long wicks show prices that were tested and rejected.

2. Stop-loss first

Decide where you will exit if the trade goes against you before entering. Never widen a stop once in a position.

3. Size the position

Risk a fixed percentage (e.g. 1%) per trade, and calculate quantity from your stop distance using the Position Size calculator.

4. Think in risk : reward

Check net R:R after STT, brokerage, and GST so transaction costs do not erode your edge.
